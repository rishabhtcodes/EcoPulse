EcoPulse India

An Environmental Intelligence and Atmospheric Monitoring Platform designed to demystify air pollution indices and deliver granular diagnostics across Indian urban centers.

Why Environmental Monitoring Matters

Air pollution represents one of the most critical public health challenges across modern India. High concentrations of inhalable particulates (PM2.5 and PM10), combined with thermal inversions, seasonal agricultural stubble burning, and vehicular congestion, severely degrade urban atmospheric conditions.

EcoPulse India bridges the communication gap between raw chemical measurements and actionable citizen intelligence by analyzing not only the overall AQI but also determining the dominant contributing pollutant and providing algorithmic public advisories.

Scoring Methodology (Eco Score)

The Environmental Score is a transparent normalized metric computed from 0 (Worst/Hazardous) to 100 (Pristine). Unlike traditional indices that simply report numbers, our algorithm weighs relative pollutant penalties against National Ambient Air Quality Standards (NAAQS):

Environmental Score = 100 - [(AQI / 500) × 65 + min(35, (PM2.5 / 250) × 35)]

This formulation penalizes acute fine particulate concentrations while maintaining smooth linear responsiveness across moderate and satisfactory air brackets.

Technology Architecture

Backend & Persistence
  • Python 3.14 + Django 6.1 framework
  • Django ORM with ForeignKey and OneToOne relationships
  • Modular decoupled apps: cities, pollution, weather, dashboard, accounts
  • Django Authentication & custom management command seed_data
Frontend & Visualization
  • Bootstrap 5 responsive UI & CSS3 custom design tokens
  • Chart.js 4.x for interactive Doughnut, Line & Bar visualizations
  • Dark and Light mode toggle with localStorage persistence
  • Asynchronous live search autocomplete endpoint
Academic & Educational Disclaimer

The data displayed in this academic project consists of simulated and modeled sample telemetry designed for class evaluation, software engineering assessment, and demonstration of full-stack Django design patterns. It should not be treated as an official government environmental monitoring source.
